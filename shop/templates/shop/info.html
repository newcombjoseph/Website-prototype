An illustrative policy for a sample store.

Demo shipping

The bag uses a $6 flat shipping estimate, with free shipping at a $75 merchandise subtotal. Prices are in USD. Demo tax is $0.00.

A real store could dispatch in 2–3 business days, subject to stock and destination. These are placeholder terms; no products will ship from this prototype.

Demo returns

A sample return window is 30 days for unused goods. This prototype does not take orders, collect addresses, issue refunds, or process returns.

Materials, pricing, tax rules, shipping zones, support details, and final policies must be supplied before a real launch.
